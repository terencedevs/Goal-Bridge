SIP IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) means contributing a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually each month. This planner estimates the monthly amount needed to work toward a goal; it does not select an investment or guarantee a return.

  • Regular contributions can make a large goal easier to plan for.
  • Your contribution is based on your goal, time horizon, existing savings, inflation, and assumed return.
  • Review the plan when your income, goal cost, or timeline changes.

CALCULATION WALKTHROUGH

How your plan is calculated

  1. Future goal cost: today’s cost is grown using your inflation assumption.
  2. Projected savings: current savings are grown using the assumed effective monthly return.
  3. Funding gap: projected savings are deducted from the future goal cost.
  4. Monthly SIP estimate: the gap is spread over the remaining months with monthly compounding.

All calculations retain precision internally and round only the displayed INR values.

SAVED SCENARIOS

HOW IT WORKS

Built for transparent decisions.

Your goal cost is grown by inflation. Existing savings and future contributions are compounded monthly using an effective monthly rate: (1 + annual return)1/12 − 1.

With a 0% return assumption, the remaining gap is simply split over the remaining months—no divide-by-zero errors.
